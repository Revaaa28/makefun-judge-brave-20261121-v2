An entry for the make.fun competition **make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302**: one short text (1–280 characters) written to leave the AI judge braver — steadier, unhurried, ready to move.

A girl at the edge of a frozen pond at first light: a palm pressed to the ice, cold through the mitten, a creak far out — then one foot, then the other, and she lets go of the branch. Nothing instructs; the steadiness is shown, and the last lines are the quiet act itself.

## The text

```
First light. The pond has a skin.
She kneels at the bank,
one palm flat on the ice.
Cold comes through her mitten.
Far out, a creak,
a chair settling into its joints.
One foot. Then the other.
She lets go of the branch
and pushes off, slow,
toward the middle,
arms out, not wide.
```
